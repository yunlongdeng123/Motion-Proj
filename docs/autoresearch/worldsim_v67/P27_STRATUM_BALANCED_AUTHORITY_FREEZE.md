# P27 Stratum-Balanced Authority Freeze

P27 rolls the P6E cache into an eleventh development domain and retrains the same bounded case-offset model. The confirmation
uses the already-consumed P6R cache and therefore carries no fresh-cohort claim.

The four groups are fixed from the existing P6R cohort order: night, rain, construction, and vulnerable transit, two scenes
each. At least 50% of evaluable cases in every group must receive authority. The total selected action count remains exactly
equal to the fixed 25% per-case baseline; P20 within-case ordering is immutable and each case may receive 0--6 actions.

Gates require exact total budget, global and per-group case coverage at least `0.50`, cost reduction at least `0.55`, delta over
fixed P20 at least `0.10`, and at least six non-increasing scenes. No group, coverage, model, offset, fraction, or gate sweep is
allowed; no hash, checksum, or fingerprint is added.
