\section{SceneIR Schema and Coordinate Conventions}

SceneIR records explicit $T_{\mathrm{city}\leftarrow\mathrm{ego}}$,
$T_{\mathrm{ego}\leftarrow\mathrm{sensor}}$, and
$T_{\mathrm{city}\leftarrow\mathrm{actor}}$ transforms. A return $x_{s,t}$ is fused in Actor coordinates as
\begin{equation}
x_i = T^{-1}_{\mathrm{city}\leftarrow\mathrm{actor},t}
      T_{\mathrm{city}\leftarrow\mathrm{ego},t}
      T_{\mathrm{ego}\leftarrow\mathrm{sensor},t} x_{s,t}.
\end{equation}
The physical layer stores Actor-local surfels, known/unknown masks, normals, temporal and view support, matched rays, and source
timestamps. Appearance primitives are referenced only through attachment provenance; Gaussian opacity is never read as collision
occupancy. Actor identity, category, extent, trajectory, lifecycle, and analytic hazard fields are immutable across compiler actions.

\paragraph{Four actions.}
\textsc{Keep} preserves supported query evidence. \textsc{Project} moves an artifact only to its matched observed termination.
\textsc{Complete} adds a canonical surfel only when temporal and view support satisfy the frozen contract. \textsc{Unknown} is the
fallback when evidence is insufficient; it never becomes free.

\section{Matched-Ray Physical Certificate}

Automotive LiDAR is 2.5D ray evidence, not merely an unordered 3D point set~\cite{hu2020visibility}. For origin $o$, direction
$v$, and observed termination $d$, the measured free interval is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal F(o,v,d)=\{o+sv\mid 0\leq s<d-\epsilon\}.
\end{equation}
A provenance-bearing \textsc{Project} candidate is placed at the same ray's termination $o+dv$. It therefore cannot lie in
$\mathcal F$. A candidate without an exact ray is marked \textsc{Unknown}; nearest-surface projection is not substituted. This is
the source of the paired zero-free-space result. It is a deterministic contract for matched rays, not a completeness proof for the
whole Actor surface or a probabilistic sensor-noise guarantee.

The held-out targets are later frames excluded from both canonical fusion and query compilation. Depth, ray-termination, Chamfer,
precision, and recall are computed against these target-only returns. This separation explains why zero paired free-space violation
can coexist with the observed \PthreeActorWorse{}\% per-Actor Chamfer tail.

\paragraph{Bidirectional visibility partition.}
For each held-out target ray, the nearest output within the frozen $0.20$\,m beam tube is classified as early, hit, or late;
otherwise it is unmatched. In the reverse direction, an output point is supported at the first return, contradicted if it lies in
the observed free interval, occluded if it is behind the return, and unknown when off ray. Occluded and unknown points are not
penalized as free-space violations, preventing unseen Actor backs from being conflated with measurable contradictions.

\paragraph{Frozen authority--certificate alignment.}
An exact identity join to the frozen P4 decisions shows why abstention must name its target. At \PtenPfourCoverage{}\%
coverage, selected Chamfer-worsening falls from \PtenAlwaysChamferWorse{}\% to \PtenSelectedChamferWorse{}\%, yet selected
visible-failure risk is \PtenSelectedVisibleRisk{}\% versus \PtenAlwaysVisibleRisk{}\% for always-repair. Its one-sided
95\% Wilson upper bound is \PtenSelectedVisibleUpper{}\%, which remains above the baseline point risk; safe-visible AUROC is
\PtenVisibleSafeAUROC{}, and abstention captures \PtenVisibleCapture{}\% of visible failures. P4 authorizes
Chamfer-nonworsening repair but provides no finite-cohort confidence separation for bidirectional visibility.

\paragraph{Why a stricter provenance rule fails.}
If authority additionally requires zero \textsc{complete} outputs, every retained point has a \textsc{keep} or matched-hit
\textsc{project} witness. Provenance-only coverage is \PelevenProvenanceCoverage{}\%; after conjunction with P4 it is
\PelevenDualCoverage{}\%. Dual visible failure falls to \PelevenDualVisibleRisk{}\% (one-sided 95\% upper
\PelevenDualVisibleUpper{}\%), but Chamfer worsening rises to \PelevenDualChamferWorse{}\% and hazardous-Actor coverage is only
\PelevenDualHazardCoverage{}\%. Completion-count unsafe-visible AUROC is \PelevenCompletionUnsafeAUROC{}. Thus observed
provenance improves one physical axis while failing joint geometry and hazard authority; it cannot certify unseen views.

\paragraph{A targeted visibility head still fails joint authority.}
A frozen nuScenes-only head reaches safe-visible AUROC \PtwelveSourceSafeAUROC{} on source test and
\PtwelveExternalSafeAUROC{} on AV2. Visibility-only AV2 selection has \PtwelveVisibilityRisk{}\% risk at
\PtwelveVisibilityCoverage{}\% coverage. Its P4 conjunction has \PtwelveDualVisibleRisk{}\% risk (95\% upper
\PtwelveDualVisibleUpper{}\%) but only \PtwelveDualCoverage{}\% coverage, \PtwelveDualChamferWorse{}\% Chamfer worsening,
and \PtwelveDualHazardCoverage{}\% hazard coverage. The ranking transfers; the authority does not.

\paragraph{Defer-to-query changes the unit of risk.}
Every abstained Actor retains its query surface, so Actor and hazard semantics remain \PthirteenActorRetention{}\%. P4's
population introduced-visible failure/composite Chamfer gain is \PthirteenPfourPopulationVisible{}\%/
\PthirteenPfourGain{}\,m; P6-C is \PthirteenPsixPopulationVisible{}\%/\PthirteenPsixGain{}\,m; and P4-and-visibility is
\PthirteenDualPopulationVisible{}\%/\PthirteenDualGain{}\,m. The provenance policy is
\PthirteenProvenancePopulationVisible{}\%/\PthirteenProvenanceGain{}\,m and is strictly dominated by query-only. Thus low
conditional risk can coexist with a worse fallback world.

\paragraph{Hazard-stratified accounting.}
For each frozen stratum, composite gain equals coverage times selected gain, while introduced-visible mass equals coverage
times selected risk (maximum numerical residual $5.55\times10^{-17}$). Hazardous Actors are
\PfourteenHazardActorShare{}\% of the cohort but account for \PfourteenPfourHazardFailureShare{}\% of P4 introduced failures
and \PfourteenPfourHazardGainShare{}\% of its Chamfer gain. P4 hazard risk is \PfourteenPfourHazardRisk{}\% versus
\PfourteenAlwaysHazardRisk{}\% for always-repair; 45 of its 48 removed failures are instead clear. P6-C repairs
\PfourteenPsixHazardCoverage{}\% of hazardous Actors at \PfourteenPsixHazardRisk{}\% risk. Conversely, P4-and-visibility's
zero observed hazardous failure covers only \PfourteenDualHazardCoverage{}\% of hazardous Actors. Thus hazard-state retention
is not a hazard-stratum visibility certificate.

\paragraph{Fresh action provenance separates two failure channels.}
Across \PfifteenFreshRays{} target rays, the compiled surface adds \PfifteenNewEarlyRate{}\% new early returns and yields
\PfifteenNewHitEarlyRatio{} new hits per new early return. COMPLETE accounts for \PfifteenCompleteNewEarlyShare{}\% of new
early returns and \PfifteenCompleteNewHitShare{}\% of new hits, whereas KEEP accounts for
\PfifteenKeepContradictionShare{}\% of emitted-surface contradictions. Hazardous/clear new-early rates are
\PfifteenHazardNewEarlyRate{}\%/\PfifteenClearNewEarlyRate{}\%; their COMPLETE hit-to-early ratios are
\PfifteenHazardCompleteHitEarly{}/\PfifteenClearCompleteHitEarly{}. P4 and P6-C hazardous rates remain
\PfifteenPfourHazardVsAlways{}$\times$/\PfifteenPsixHazardVsAlways{}$\times$ always-repair. Thus the selectors do not
suppress the completion-driven hazardous-ray mechanism. PROJECT has zero emitted provenance because observed-hit projections
collapse under KEEP-first voxel deduplication; this is not a causal zero-effect result.

\section{Literal First-Return Correction and Monotone Boundary}

The frozen AV2 attribution above selects the Euclidean-nearest output to each target point and then checks its projected depth. It
is a target-aligned proximity diagnostic, not literal ray termination. Following raw-LiDAR occupancy evaluation
~\cite{kalble2024accurate}, P20 instead takes the minimum positive projected depth among every surface point inside the same frozen
beam tube. Query and compiled surfaces use the identical operator.

\begin{equation}
d_S(r)=\min\{s>0:o+sv\in S_{\epsilon}\},\qquad \min\varnothing=\infty.
\label{eq:supp-first-return}
\end{equation}

For any $S'\subseteq S$, Eq.~\ref{eq:supp-first-return} gives $d_{S'}(r)\ge d_S(r)$, so deletion cannot increase the early predicate or
new-early relative to a fixed query. There is no corresponding guarantee for hits or symmetric Chamfer. Table~\ref{tab:supp-true-ray}
shows exactly this boundary on the consumed nuScenes source-development cohort: all three frozen deletion policies reduce hazardous
early returns, yet all pay surface utility. P19 is most efficient, removing \PtwentyPnineteenHazardRemoved{} hazardous events for
\PtwentyPnineteenHitsLost{} hits and \PtwentyPnineteenPenaltyMM{}\,mm mean Chamfer
(\PtwentyPnineteenEventsPerHit{} events/hit; \PtwentyPnineteenEventsPerMM{} events/mm). These ratios are descriptive, not
collision or road-safety guarantees.

\section{Actor-Level Decision Intervals}

For the frozen ReLU validity MLP, an input box $[\ell,u]$ is propagated through each affine layer using
\begin{align}
\ell' &= W^+\ell + W^-u + b, &
u' &= W^+u + W^-\ell + b,
\end{align}
followed by endpoint-wise ReLU. Here $W^+=\max(W,0)$ and $W^-=\min(W,0)$. The resulting logit interval is mapped through the
sigmoid. An Actor is \emph{robust-select} if its lower probability is above the frozen P4 threshold, \emph{robust-abstain} if its
upper probability is below threshold, and unresolved otherwise. This is deterministic interval-bound propagation over a specified
box, related to robust-OOD certification~\cite{bitterwolf2020certifiably}; it does not assert that the box describes an unseen sensor.

We fix radii $0.05/0.10/0.20$ in nuScenes-training standard deviations. The sensor-opportunity group contains point/surfel counts,
temporal/view support, range, and frame count; the physical-surface group contains surface residuals, keep/unknown fractions,
completion fraction, and compiled/query ratio. Their union is exactly the 13D validity input.

At radius $0.10$, \PsevenCTestCertified{}\% of nominal nuScenes-test selections and \PsevenCAVCertified{}\% of consumed-AV2
selections are robust-select. Nevertheless, \PsevenCAVStableFalse{}\% of the latter are retained target false repairs. Query-point
count is the largest one-feature interval source for \PsevenCTestQueryTop{}\%/\PsevenCAVQueryTop{}\% of nuScenes/AV2 Actors.
Thus a shifted model can be confidently and locally stable while wrong.

\section{Resource and Reproducibility Boundary}

All V7 scientific runs use one RTX~3090. The largest paper-facing GPU allocation is below 1.5\,GiB, while P7-C uses 9.2\,MiB and
1.37\,s. Download and preprocessing are sequential on the low-memory host. Artifacts are identified by task/run paths and immutable
cohort/config files; the protocol intentionally does not add hashes, checksums, fingerprints, repeated smoke matrices, or post-hoc
quality gates.
