\begin{abstract}
Driving-world reconstruction can appear safer by removing uncertain Actors, including the legitimate hazards that matter
for evaluation. We instead define physical consistency from ground-truth ray and surface supervision while keeping Actor
identity, trajectory, extent, and hazard state immutable. Our compiler first separates image-rendering Gaussians from an
Actor-canonical physical surface and exposes observed free, occupied, and unknown evidence without turning unknown mass
into a deletion rule. On frozen Argoverse~2 data, the non-learned compiler reduces LiDAR depth error from 0.207 to
0.154\,m and symmetric Chamfer from 0.254 to 0.175\,m while retaining every Actor and hazard state. We then train geometry
on nuScenes: each completion seed generates a small set of child surfaces under motion-corrected target-set, local-plane,
scale, per-frame coverage, literal first-return, and free-before-hit losses. With no deployment filter, this model reduces
hazardous early returns by 5.12\%, improves Chamfer by 6.21\,mm, and raises hit recall by 2.76 points on an exposed
development fold. Frozen producer evidence further predicts continuous FREE/OCCUPIED/UNKNOWN authority. Interpreting that
authority as a categorical surface-return measure reduces hazardous early returns by 0.62 points and raises hazardous hit
recall by 2.32 points relative to unit energy; treating it as additive opacity fails. Finally, we derive an exact boundary
for normalized mixtures: attenuating component $j$ lowers the pre-hit CDF iff its component CDF exceeds the current
mixture CDF, and attenuation magnitude cannot reverse this sign. A failed learned-visibility ablation raises the boundary
CDF on 59.32\% of 99,208 rays, matching the predicted non-monotonicity. Typed ownership and inverse-query SE(3)
composition keep appearance, canonical geometry, and rigid motion structurally separate. The preregistered cross-sensor
aggregate remains incomplete and is excluded from all claims. We therefore claim source-domain supervised physical
completion and a safety boundary, not domain invariance or real-road safety.
\end{abstract}
