\section{Current Method: Reproducibility Details}
\label{sec:archive-repro}

\subsection{Data Roles and Denominators}

The V7.1 corpus contains 1,004 Actors from raw and role-separated processed nuScenes producers.
Candidate-bearing preparation supplies the 659 Actors used in the later geometry/evidence stages.
The deterministic index-modulo-ten split yields 593 training and 66 development Actors.
Parent-representation exposure predates the latter split, so the 66-Actor results are development evidence.
The earlier S1 oracle uses 64 training Actors; M0/M1 use 88 source-selection Actors; B4 uses 121.
The different eligibility conditions explain these different denominators.

Build observations and target observations are separated by sweep role. Both sensor origins and endpoints
are transformed into each Actor's canonical coordinates.
Inputs retain only build-side evidence; disjoint target geometry supplies losses and evaluation labels.
For AV2, the frozen compiler uses every third evaluation sweep with target modulo value two,
at least four build frames and three target frames, and the eight configured rigid-vehicle categories.

The frozen external cohort contains 116 hazardous and 236 clear Actors.
Its 466,576 hazardous and 550,076 clear rays sum to 1,016,652.
The source fold contains 82,444 hazardous and 16,764 clear rays.
We aggregate ray counts before division, including unmatched rays in the denominator.
Chamfer averages the per-Actor symmetric nearest-neighbor distance.
Frame coverage averages target points within frame, frames within Actor, and Actors within the reported group.

Hazard is a frozen Actor-level annotation derived from the supplied motion and ego interaction:
short time to collision, small minimum clearance, interaction-range hard braking, or crossing.
It is independent of the learned surface and is never an input or optimization target.
The frozen criteria are TTC at most 3\,s, clearance at most 2\,m, normalized hard-brake score at least
$.75$ within 15\,m, or crossing within 5\,m clearance and a 20\,m forward window.
Hard braking is normalized by $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$; category mapping remains in the inherited compiler configuration.

\subsection{Geometry Schedule}

A frozen parent relocation model (M5) supplies the coordinate base.
M7 expands each parent into four learned slots with ten geometry epochs followed by eight geometry/ray epochs
(seed 71109, learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$).
M8 initializes from M7 and adds equal-frame target coverage for six epochs.
Its seed is 71110, hidden width 128, slot dimension 16, Actor batch size four, AdamW learning rate
$10^{-4}$, and weight decay $10^{-4}$.
Centers receive per-axis bounded $0.25$\,m residuals and are restricted by the configured padded cuboid.
Scales are isotropic, bounded to $[0.02,0.40]$\,m, and initialized at $0.10$\,m.
Eight nearest target points define local planes and spacing targets.

Training uses at most 512 target rays and 512 anchors per Actor.
Frame coverage samples at most 256 rays per target-origin group.
The set, plane, scale, first-depth, and pre-hit hinge losses are divided by detached parent-reference
values clamped below by $10^{-3}$; M8 frame distance is normalized by its frozen M7 reference.
Geometry weights are $1,.25,.10$, and the frame term has weight one.
Ray depth has weight one and the pre-hit hinge has weight $.5$.
Two-task PCGrad operates on the geometry-plus-frame and ray gradients.

The smooth renderer uses 48 depth samples, fixed $0.08$\,m support width, density multiplier two,
and a residual depth $0.50$\,m behind the target.
These fixed renderer widths differ from the learned primitive scales used by the categorical field.
The training ray sampler depends on target depth as part of constructing the loss; inference samples
the annotated Actor-box interval instead.
Deployment emits all predicted children with all anchors and performs the fixed $0.06$\,m voxel deduplication.
It applies no learned evidence threshold.

\subsection{Evidence Schedule and Categorical Inference}

M33's sidecar aligns 297,535 observed anchors by producer identity. The heads retain canonical geometry,
construction provenance, source ray/frame descriptors, projection displacement, support counts, and
build free/occupied/unknown evidence. Children inherit their parent's build evidence and child geometry.
Each head uses a two-layer primitive encoder with ReLU, max-pooled Actor context, and a three-logit
evidence output of hidden width 64.

M35 trains the anchor head for six epochs (seed 71135). M37 trains a separate child head with the
anchor head frozen, and M38 fine-tunes it for four epochs (seed 71138) with integrated pre-hit survival.
Evidence stages use Actor batches of four, learning rate $5\times10^{-4}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$,
at most 128 training rays, and 32 transmittance segments.
The return objective combines segment negative log-likelihood, expected-depth L1 with weight $.10$,
and soft evidence cross-entropy with weight $.50$.
M38 adds child optical thickness before $d^\star-.20$\,m with weight one.
Its fixed geometry and anchor head are unchanged.

M39 performs no optimization. It loads M8, M35, and M38, evaluates occupied weights through the same
Gaussian kernel, and normalizes joint depth--primitive mass.
Evaluation uses 64 samples in the Actor box padded by $.15$\,m and a median level of $.50$.
Observed anchors have fixed $.08$\,m scale; children use the learned M8 scale.
A small positive weight floor is used for log-domain numerical stability.
The median linearly interpolates between the previous and first crossing CDF samples; at the first
sample the left depth is the box entry. All evaluated M39 rays are observable in both datasets.

For finite point set $X$ and unit direction $v$, the literal evaluator computes
\begin{equation}
d_X(r)=\min_{\substack{x\in X,\;s_x>0\\
\|x-o-s_xv\|\leq\epsilon}}s_x,\qquad
s_x=(x-o)^\top v,
\label{eq:archive-literal}
\end{equation}
with $\epsilon=.20$\,m and $\min\varnothing=\infty$.
This is a minimum over projected centers within a cylindrical beam; it is not an analytic Gaussian
entry depth or the first intersection with a sphere.
The categorical operator weights continuous Gaussian support on sampled depths and interpolates its median.
The shared event tolerance is $.20$\,m.

The source M8 absolute early rates are 25.70/26.37/22.39\% for all/hazardous/clear Actors.
Its clear-stratum regression is retained in the main table.
The source M39 measure improves all three strata over unit energy, with 100\% observability.
The two source comparisons evaluate different operators and different interventions:
M8 changes centers; M39 changes weights on fixed centers and scales.
