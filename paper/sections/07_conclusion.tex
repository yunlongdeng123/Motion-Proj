\section{Conclusion}

We formulate driving-world compilation around a simple boundary: validity is not difficulty. \method repairs evidence-inconsistent physical geometry while preserving legitimate hazardous Actors and factorizes repairability from hazard with explicit abstention. Frozen nuScenes-to-AV2 transfer reduces false repair without using hazard inputs or deleting hazardous Actors. Its safety-envelope audit further turns an abstract guarantee gap into a measurable boundary: task leakage is structurally zero, yet cross-domain validity relies strongly on sensor opportunity. The resulting SceneIR keeps appearance, collision state, unknown space, provenance, and task reliability explicit. Remaining work targets opportunity-normalized transfer, the continuous multi-horizon reliability layer, and fresh external domains without expanding the demonstrated claim boundary.
