\section{Conclusion}

We formulate driving-world compilation around a simple boundary: validity is not difficulty. \method repairs evidence-inconsistent physical geometry while preserving legitimate hazardous Actors and factorizes repairability from hazard with explicit abstention. Frozen nuScenes-to-AV2 transfer reduces false repair without using hazard inputs or deleting hazardous Actors, while the calibration coverage shift makes the external guarantee gap visible rather than hiding it. The resulting SceneIR keeps appearance, collision state, unknown space, provenance, and task reliability explicit. Remaining work targets the continuous multi-horizon reliability layer and broader external domains without expanding the demonstrated claim boundary.
