\section{Conclusion}

\method separates validity, hazard, and task authority. Literal audits expose proxy undercounting and deletion
monotonicity---not road-safety guarantees. A continuous-displacement oracle demonstrates that joint first-return and surface
improvement is geometrically possible, while fixed learned displacement and implicit-field models do not recover that point on
source selection. Their collapse toward missing surface, together with the converse TSDF endpoint, makes the safety--completeness
boundary explicit. Withholding source-final and external evaluation after this rejection preserves that negative conclusion:
physical plausibility requires jointly audited geometry and ray evidence, not a single improved metric.
