\section{Limitations}

The frozen AV2 attribution is a target-nearest proxy. A later consumed-source audit corrects it to literal first return, but
cannot retroactively make the AV2 metric pre-registered. Point deletion certifies only non-increasing early returns---not hit
retention, Chamfer, collision freedom, or a safe policy. \method is therefore not a real-road safety guarantee. Sparse sensing
leaves unknown space, tracked cuboids may be wrong, and only 64.0\% of Actors add no observed-ray violation. Visibility-driven
abstention reduces point risk by sacrificing geometry or hazardous-Actor coverage. The selector has only 29 admissible source
training Actors; its 8.9\%$\rightarrow$75.6\% coverage shift, source--target rank reversal, and 49.5\% AV2 sensitivity to
observation-frame count rule out cross-domain exchangeability. Exact reliability alignment supplies only five selected test
Actors, while the composed audit reuses source outcomes without executing vehicle dynamics; it shows interface compatibility,
not collision or policy improvement. Static topology, deformable Actors, measured sensor-error sets, closed-loop dynamics, and
appearance-dependent hazard remain open.
