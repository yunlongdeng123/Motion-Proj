\section{Problem Formulation}

Actor $i$ has immutable state $A_i=(ID_i,T_{i,0:H},D_i,C_i)$: identity, rigid pose trajectory, dimensions, and category.
Build sweeps provide canonicalized endpoints and FREE/OCCUPIED/UNKNOWN ray evidence $B_i$; disjoint target sweeps provide
GT surface and first-return supervision $Y_i$ but are never model inputs. The learned physical state is
$P_i=(S_i,m_i)$, comprising an Actor-canonical surface and continuous primitive evidence. A read-only pose $T_{it}$ places
$P_i$ in the world. Static Background and visual state $V_i$ have separate owners.

We seek parameters $(\theta,\phi)$ for geometry $S_i^\theta(B_i)$ and evidence $m_i^\phi(B_i)$ that jointly improve
held-out surface distance and literal first-return behavior,
\begin{equation}
\min_{\theta,\phi}\ \mathbb E_i\!\left[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{surface}}(S_i^\theta,Y_i)+
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{return}}(S_i^\theta,m_i^\phi,Y_i)
\right],
\label{eq:problem-objective}
\end{equation}
subject to three deployment invariants: every generated surface remains queryable; $A_i$ is unchanged; and the physical query
has no argument or gradient path from $V_i$. A lower early-return rate alone is insufficient, because deleting a surface can
reduce early events while degrading hit recall and Chamfer. We therefore require the source-domain direction to improve
hazard-stratified early returns without sacrificing surface fidelity or Actor retention, and evaluate the frozen representation
once on AV2 without target adaptation.
