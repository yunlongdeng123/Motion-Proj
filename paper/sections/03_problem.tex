\section{Problem Formulation}

Let Actor $i$ have an immutable semantic state
\begin{equation}
A_i=(ID_i,X_{i,0:H},D_i),
\end{equation}
containing identity, trajectory, and dimensions. Appearance primitives may be associated with $A_i$, but collision queries consume only an Actor-local physical surface $S_i^{\mathrm{phys}}$ and its known/unknown mask.

We separate two random variables. Artifactness $a_i$ depends on sensor and geometric evidence $E_i$, physical surface state, and provenance:
\begin{equation}
a_i=P(\mathrm{artifact}\mid E_i,S_i^{\mathrm{phys}},P_i).
\end{equation}
Hazardness $h_i$ depends on Actor dynamics, map context, and a candidate Ego trajectory $\tau$:
\begin{equation}
h_i=P(\mathrm{hazard}\mid X_{i,0:H},\tau,\mathcal{M}).
\end{equation}
The goal is paired conditional invariance, not unconditional statistical independence. For the same clean Actor, a legal safe-to-hazard intervention should preserve $a_i$. For the same behavior, a clean-to-artifact intervention should preserve $h_i$.

For task reliability, let $n_{\tau,t}$ be the local boundary normal, $d_{i,t}(\tau)$ the signed Actor clearance, and $\epsilon_{i,t}$ the Actor residual. We define
\begin{equation}
C(\tau,H)=\max_{i,t\le H}\frac{|n_{\tau,t}^{\top}\epsilon_{i,t}|}{\max(|d_{i,t}(\tau)|,\varepsilon)}.
\label{eq:boundary-cost}
\end{equation}
The runtime interface returns $R(\tau,H,b)=P(C\le b)$ and enforces $\partial R/\partial b\ge0$ and $\partial R/\partial H\le0$.
