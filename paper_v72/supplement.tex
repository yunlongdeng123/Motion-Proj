\section{Data and Evaluation Protocol}
The clean split is selected from dependency-chain-unexposed nuScenes logs using metadata
only: four development logs, three route-selection logs, and three unopened source-test
candidates. Selective archive I/O materializes 3,882 development and route-selection
keyframe scans (2,695,516,800 bytes). Only development is compiled and evaluated after
the gate is frozen. It contains 65 scenes, 501 Actors, 2,059,365 build points, and
641,930 held-out positive-return rays. Actor bundles preserve point/ray provenance,
intensity, ring, time, and poses; target arrays are stored separately.

\section{Formal Runs}
The AdaPoinTr transfer baseline uses the official PCN checkpoint, followed by 600 epochs
of legacy adaptation. A1 starts from that frozen checkpoint and runs 100 additional
epochs with surface, hit, and known-free losses. Its checkpoint SHA-256 is
\texttt{d489e2bb...e2d7}; the full digest is retained in the run manifest.
The clean development evaluator compares raw fusion, Actor TSDF, adapted AdaPoinTr,
unanchored A1, and the fixed anchored output at 64, 128, 256, 512, and native budgets.
Every run records a resolved configuration, manifest, status, Actor and log rows,
aggregate summary, resource use, data roles, and failure-ledger references.

\section{D1 Gate}
At the 512-point cap, Actor TSDF and the anchored candidate obtain CD
.178599/.194976, F-score .750319/.714542, early rate .411462/.439344, and hit recall
.549722/.522150. The candidate therefore misses both primary-effect alternatives and
both side-effect limits. The mechanical result is
\texttt{decision=close\_method\_claim}. Route selection, source test, and external test
remain unread, and no P3/P4 experiment is authorized.

\section{Failure and Scope Boundary}
\texttt{V71-F62} records the recoverable clean-data payload assumption; selective
official-archive extraction resolves it without changing the split. \texttt{V71-F63}
records the scientific D1 rejection. The latter is not repaired with an anchor-fraction,
loss-weight, density, or threshold sweep. LiDAR4D demonstrates full-scan capability on
its native KITTI-360 protocol, but its absolute numbers are not compared with the Actor
task.

\section{Reproducibility Checklist}
The final role state is: legacy diagnostic and clean development read; route selection,
source test, and external test unread. Data, method, and metric artifacts are linked in
\texttt{docs/WORLDSIM\_V7\_2\_D1\_NEGATIVE\_CLOSEOUT.md}. The main paper and this
supplement describe a negative technical report rather than a submission-ready method.
