\section{Data and I/O Contract}
Every Actor artifact separates build inputs from target arrays and stores scene, log,
track, pose, and measurement provenance. The route role contains three logs, 31 scenes,
310 Actors, 816,442 build points, and 284,030 target rays. The source role contains three
logs and 17 scenes. Selective extraction from official nuScenes shards 02 and 10 recovers
677/677 required source keyframes (470,123,520 bytes). Source ActorBundleV2 and EAS
corpora independently materialize the same 80 Actors and share fingerprint
\texttt{2ec626e1...fcc4c1c}; 50 are hazardous, with 265,177 build points and 94,615 query
rays. RGB recovery extracts only predeclared JPEG paths and records archive and image
SHA-256 values.

allocated GPU memory.

\section{Frozen Source Outcome}
The source cache processes the exact 12 locked sample tokens and peaks at 7.61 GiB. It
finds eight Actor-pose matches but no candidate with a camera observation. The formal
evaluator reaches all four arms, whose evidence tensors are empty, then correctly
refuses to serialize non-finite means. The failed attempt is retained. A separate
target-free support audit verifies the original lock and writes the canonical verdict
\path{frozen_confirmation_inconclusive_insufficient_visual_support}; it loads no
checkpoint or label and performs no selection. A future evaluation must freeze a
minimum camera/Actor support rule before opening source quality.

\section{Failure Boundaries}
Engineering recoveries include official RGB and LiDAR selective I/O, dtype conversion
for MapAnything camera prompts, and strict support reporting for empty cohorts. Scientific
negative results remain visible: early visual fusion overfits, $\pi^3$ visual evidence
hurts on development, raw VGGT fusion harms route Brier, and the frozen source set is
not evaluable. None is converted into a positive claim by changing scenes or thresholds.
