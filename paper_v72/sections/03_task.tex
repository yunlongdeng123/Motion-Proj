\section{Task Definition}
For Actor $i$, build observations provide calibrated LiDAR rays and RGB views, an
identity, and known poses $T_i(t)\in\mathrm{SE}(3)$. Held-out points, ray outcomes, and
target RGB are labels and never enter the forward pass. The representation is
\[
S_i=(P_i,E_i,A_i,T_i),
\]
where $P_i$ is an Actor-canonical physical surface, $E_i$ is continuous measurement
evidence, and $A_i$ is appearance. For a world ray $q=(o,d,r_{\min},r_{\max})$, the
model predicts a normalized distribution over ordered surface returns and no-return.

The intended task is sparse-build dynamic reconstruction and sensor replay under a
given trajectory. It is not future trajectory prediction. Invalid or unfired queries,
valid no-returns, unsupported geometry, background events, and Actor events remain
separate. Geometry is evaluated independently from the return reader so a learned
decoder cannot hide a weak surface.
