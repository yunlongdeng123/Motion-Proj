\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Feed-forward visual geometry.}
VGGT jointly predicts cameras, depth, point maps, and tracks from multiple
views~\cite{wang2025vggt}. $\pi^3$ removes a distinguished reference view through
permutation-equivariant geometry prediction~\cite{wang2025pi3}. MapAnything accepts
optional intrinsics, poses, depth, and metric-scale prompts in a unified
model~\cite{keetha2025mapanything}. These are strong geometry priors, but none directly
defines sensor free/occupied/unknown evidence or an ordered first-return distribution.

\paragraph{Robust multimodal fusion.}
Fusion may fail when one modality is missing or degraded. Work on missing-modality
transformers~\cite{ma2022missing} and multi-modal expert routing~\cite{park2025mome}
motivates keeping visual and measurement experts separately usable. Our reliability
discount is a small post-hoc instance of this principle; it is selected only on route
data and does not constitute a new general calibration method.

\paragraph{Neural LiDAR and dynamic rendering.}
LiDAR4D models geometry, intensity, ray drop, and dynamic scenes in a neural
field~\cite{zheng2024lidar4d}. Gaussian-splatting systems provide high-quality RGB
appearance but their opacity is a rendering quantity. EAS instead keeps a sensor
evidence measure, an appearance state, and an Actor trajectory as distinct objects.
