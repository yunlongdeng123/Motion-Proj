\begin{abstract}
Dynamic LiDAR world simulation requires more than completing an actor point cloud:
it must predict observable returns without consulting held-out targets, preserve
visibility and no-return semantics, and compose actors with the static scene under a
single depth rule. We formulate this task through an evidential surface interface and
evaluate two candidate research routes: object-level surface completion and full neural
LiDAR simulation. Quantitative claims are withheld until the preregistered D0 baseline
and route-selection gate is complete.
\end{abstract}
