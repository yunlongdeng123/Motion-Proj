\section{Limitations}
Only four fully isolated source logs are available for development, so uncertainty
across driving domains is not resolved. We intentionally do not read the three
route-selection logs or source-test candidates after the development failure. The
object task contains positive held-out returns and measured pre-hit free samples, but it
does not support a full volumetric occupancy claim. LiDAR4D is evaluated on its native
KITTI-360 protocol; its absolute metrics are not comparable with the object table. The
study therefore validates the audit, data, and baseline infrastructure while providing
no competitive method, application, generalization, occupancy, or safety claim.
