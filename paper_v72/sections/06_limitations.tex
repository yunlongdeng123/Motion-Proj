\section{Limitations}
The positive real-data gain is small and appears in evidence calibration, while the
physical surface is unchanged. $\pi^3$ does not reproduce the VGGT gain, and the
MapAnything run is a diagnostic rather than an adapted EAS baseline. The ordered-return
result uses a controlled analytic generator; real valid no-return and background
composition still require a matched full-scene dataset. Appearance improves only one
Actor sequence and remains well below the StreetGS reference. Exact SE(3) identities
validate the representation, not pose estimation or future-motion prediction.

Most consequentially, the frozen source scenes have no visual candidate support. A
future independent protocol must predeclare a target-free eligibility threshold for
camera/Actor overlap and evaluate the fallback expert when a modality is absent. It
must then freeze that rule before opening quality. The current source data cannot be
reused to claim independent confirmation. No occupancy, complete waveform, collision,
planning, closed-loop, or safety claim follows from these experiments.
