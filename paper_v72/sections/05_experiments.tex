\section{Experiments}
\paragraph{Data and protocol.}
nuScenes logs are separated into fit/development, route-selection, and source roles.
The route corpus contains 31 scenes and 310 Actors (142 hazardous), with 816,442 build
points and 284,030 target rays. Its visual evaluation covers 35 Actors and 239 surface
candidates. The source I/O recovery materializes all 677 required keyframe scans and
80 Actors from three frozen logs. Model selection never uses source labels.

\paragraph{Foundation diagnostics.}
Official VGGT-1B and $\pi^3$ checkpoints run through one shared camera/point-map
interface. Their first raw LiDAR-alignment diagnostic gives median visible-surface
residuals of 74.67 m and 3.41 m, respectively, after the then-current similarity
alignment. These values diagnose adapter/alignment risk rather than benchmark the
backbones. MapAnything is then run through its native calibrated pose and intrinsic
prompts on a route window; it reaches 0.799 m median surface residual and 4.76\% hit at
0.2 m. It is a stronger metric reference, but still does not supply range-sensor
evidence directly.

\paragraph{Late fusion is the transferable development result.}
Table~\ref{tab:e2-dev} shows that the original joint adapter overfits relative to the
late-fusion decomposition. A near-capacity scalar head also generalizes poorly. The
VGGT visual residual improves Brier by .00431 over its no-visual counterpart while
surface displacement RMSE is effectively unchanged (.07530 versus .07549 m). Replacing
VGGT with $\pi^3$ worsens Brier from .19264 to .24066, so cross-backbone universality is
not supported.

\paragraph{Reliability discount.}
Raw visual fusion slightly improves route NLL but harms Brier. Selecting $\alpha=.35$
on route data gives the best Brier among the fixed grid and improves both proper scores
over the no-visual expert (Table~\ref{tab:e5-route}); two of three logs improve in
Brier. Mean surface F-score remains .66756 and symmetric CD remains approximately
.21031 m for every learned arm. This is evidence calibration, not geometry repair.

\paragraph{Composition mechanisms.}
The separated blocking/detection reader remains better after moving the front Actor
behind the background (NLL .67563 versus .93539), and mass splitting changes the output
by zero numerically. The appearance bridge assigns all 309 physical parents to 3,090
visual primitives. Across six held-out views it improves pooled Actor PSNR from 17.186
to 17.730 dB; physical geometry and trajectory receive no RGB update. Its 25.35 dB
StreetGS reference also records the remaining appearance gap. On 11 Actors and 40
poses, maximum SE(3) commutation, Actor-frame recovery, and pairwise-distance errors are
$6.82\times10^{-13}$, $1.48\times10^{-13}$, and $2.82\times10^{-13}$ m; the largest
observed translation span is 5.24 m.

\paragraph{Source support and neural-LiDAR boundary.}
The frozen source visual set contains 12 windows and eight matched Actor poses, but zero
observable surface candidates and zero camera observations. Proper evidence scores are
therefore undefined. A target-free audit records
\texttt{inconclusive\_insufficient\_visual\_support}; the route result is not promoted
to an independent-generalization claim. Separately, an official LiDAR4D capability run
on KITTI-360 completes range, ray-drop, intensity, and scan export (ray-drop F1 .9553,
depth RMSE 2.947 m). Its native protocol is not numerically comparable to the Actor task.
