\section{Conclusion}
EAS-VGGT reorganizes dynamic world simulation around owned physical state, continuous
measurement evidence, independent appearance, and a shared rigid trajectory. The
prototype establishes four concrete mechanisms: late visual evidence adaptation,
ordered blocking/detection composition, gradient-isolated appearance with parent
correspondence, and exact SE(3) action. The real-data evidence gain is modest and does
not improve geometry, while source confirmation lacks support. The next publishable
step is consequently clear: learn localized physical surface events on a support-eligible
independent cohort and compare them under a complete first-return protocol.
